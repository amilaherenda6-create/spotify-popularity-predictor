The Problem

Every year, millions of songs are uploaded to Spotify. Most are never heard. A handful become global hits.

The question every artist, producer, and label manager asks:

  • 🎯 Will this song be a hit? (Yes or No)
  • 📊 How popular will it be? (on a scale of 0 to 100)

Our approach: Analyse only the audio characteristics of a song — no artist name, no label, no marketing budget.

If the sound itself contains the secret to success, our tool will find it.

The Data

What we built the model on

  • 114,000 Spotify tracks
  • 21 audio features per song
  • 114 music genres

Key signal set

Feature Why it matters
Danceability Higher values often mean stronger playlist appeal
Energy Intense songs tend to attract more listeners
Loudness Louder tracks stand out in stream-heavy playlists
Valence Mood and emotion influence sharing and engagement
Tempo Faster beats perform better in pop and dance genres
Acousticness Lower values usually align with mainstream hits

Our Approach

A practical prediction workflow

  • Clean the dataset and filter unusable records
  • Train classification and regression models
  • Compare against simple baselines
  • Deploy a live app for real-time scoring

Classification Goal

Can this song be popular?

  • We define a hit as popularity ≥ 70
  • The model outputs a yes/no recommendation
  • This is a fast filter for release decisions

Regression Goal

How popular will the song score?

  • Predicts Spotify popularity on a 0–100 scale
  • Gives a more nuanced view than binary hit/no-hit
  • Helps compare tracks within the same release batch

Data Insights

  • Only 1 in 20 songs reaches the hit threshold (score ≥ 70)
  • Most songs cluster between 20 and 40 popularity points
  • Loud, energetic, danceable tracks perform better
  • Genre is the strongest single predictor

What Matters Most

  • Genre is the most powerful signal
  • Loudness and energy consistently help tracks score higher
  • Danceability supports playlist performance
  • Acoustic, slow, and instrumental songs tend to score lower

Deployment

  • Streamlit app for live song scoring
  • FastAPI backend for model serving
  • Simple audio-feature input form
  • Clear results for artists and label teams

Limitations

  • Cannot predict viral social momentum
  • Does not measure artistic quality
  • Is weaker for niche genres
  • Should not replace expert human judgment

Recommendations

  • Use this tool as an early screening filter
  • Prioritise energetic, danceable tracks for mainstream appeal
  • Combine model output with creative review
  • Treat prediction as guidance, not the final decision
